- Memory: handoff note (`agent/HANDOFF.md`) and the outcomes block from closed trades; a lesson changes a call only with at least 5 supporting closed trades.

## Baselines (computed offline from history and the same cost model, no live code)
Random calls with the same number of long entries, horizons and costs (300 draws, median and 5th to 95th percentile); equal-weight basket of the traded universe rebalanced monthly; cash.

## Pass bar (fixed now)
After at least 60 trading days and at least 30 closed trades, the account passes only if all hold: net return after costs above the median of random calls and above the equal-weight basket; still positive after removing the best single trade; mean net return per trade positive with a bootstrap 90% interval above zero; higher-confidence trades do better than lower-confidence ones (rank correlation above zero). Otherwise the answer is "no edge shown", which is a valid result. Early looks are reported but do not stop or change the test.

## Not allowed during the test
Changing the universe, limits, costs, prompts or models without writing the change and its date in `PROGRESS_LOG.md`; results before the change then count as a separate period. Looking at outcomes to choose names.

## Known caveats
The agent may recall history (all days are after its knowledge cutoff, but its knowledge of company facts is older). One account, one market regime, about three months: low power by design; a pass would call for a longer second test, not for real money.

## Pass bar from period 3 on (rewrites the bar above; reason: 30 trades can only show an edge of about 4% a trade, see `results/EU_SCREEN_TEST.md` power table)
Primary evidence is scored on many calls, not on a few trades:
1. **Shadow calls on all watchlist names** (once built: one direction call per name per morning run; until then the existing shadow list). Scored at the horizon from daily closes, net of the table's round-trip cost, against the equal-weight watchlist basket over the same days. Pass: after at least 60 trading days, mean net excess of "up" calls above zero with a month-clustered t of at least 2, and "up" minus "down" spread above zero; not driven by one month (still positive without the best month).
2. **News ablation** (once built): the same S1 screen candidates judged with and without news; news passes only if the with-news calls beat the without-news calls on the same days by the rule in 1.
3. **S1 forward log** (`src/screen_log.py`): reported, judged at about 125 independent 20-day cases.
4. **The account itself:** reported every day against random calls on the watchlist (same number of longs, horizons, costs), the equal-weight basket, a trend-filtered basket and EUNL.DE buy-and-hold; it passes only if 1 passes AND its net return beats the random-call median and the basket after at least 60 trading days. Open-universe trades are compared with EXW1.DE over the same days.
A pass leads to a longer test on fresh days, never straight to real money.
